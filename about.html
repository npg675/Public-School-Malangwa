About the School

Shree Public Secondary School is a public, co-educational school located in Malangwa Municipality-2, Sarlahi, Madhesh Province. The school provides education from Early Childhood Development through Grade 12, serving more than one thousand students (1,085 in 2081/82; 1,216 in 2080/81 per IEMIS/CEHRD via Edusanjal).

Along with basic and secondary education, the school offers +2 programs in Science and Management under Nepal's National Examinations Board. As a community educational institution in Malangwa, the school also participates in educational and social awareness activities serving students and the wider community.
