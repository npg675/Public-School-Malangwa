## About the School
**Shree Public Secondary School** is a public, co-educational community school located in **Malangwa Municipality-2, Sarlahi, Madhesh Province**. The school provides education from **Early Childhood Development through Grade 12**, serving **more than one thousand students** (1,085 in 2081/82; 1,216 in 2080/81 per IEMIS/CEHRD via Edusanjal).

Along with basic and secondary education, the school offers **+2 programs in Science and Management** under Nepal's **National Examinations Board (NEB)**. As a community educational institution in Malangwa, the school also participates in educational and social awareness activities serving students and the wider community — e.g., the **16-Day Campaign Against Gender-Based Violence** program inaugurated at **Shri Public Secondary School, Malangwa-2** in November 2025 with Malangwa Municipality, INSEC and local groups, and a **June 8, 2025 CSR disclosure** referencing a project/activity at **Shree Public Secondary School, Malangwa-2, Sarlahi** (Laxmi Sunrise Bank).
