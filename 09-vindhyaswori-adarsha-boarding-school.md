## About the School
Vindhyaswori Adarsha Boarding School is a private educational institution established in 1984 AD (2041 BS) and situated in Mohariya Tole, Pokhara-1, Kaski, Gandaki Province. According to its directory tagline, it positions itself as "A complete educational institution which dedicated to Quality education, discipline & wisdom." The school is affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB) and approved by the Ministry of Education.

The school provides education up to secondary level (Grade 10 / SEE) and also runs Ten Plus Two (Class 11 and 12) programs, offering a continuous schooling pathway in one location. CollegeNP describes it as providing education "with moderate fee structures" and operating a scholarship scheme for deserving students.

Alumni and staff traces confirm the school has been operating for decades — one former teacher lists the school on LinkedIn as his workplace from Nov 2016 to Jan 2018, and an alumnus lists his SLC (School Leaving Certificate) years as 1995–2005, indicating the school has been serving the Mohariya community since the 1990s. The school has also posted teacher and accountant job vacancies through CollegeNP (most recently a teacher vacancy in April 2023), signalling an active hiring and operations cycle.

Despite this long history, the school's digital footprint is minimal and fragmented. It is listed on several education directories (CollegeNP, Edusanjal, Suchanaa, SchoolAndCollegeListings, OpenAlfa) but none of those profiles are claimed or actively managed by the school, and all show zero reviews. The one asset it officially owns — the domain vindhyaswori.edu.np — is currently unreachable, meaning the school has no single authoritative online home. This is the central modernization opportunity: consolidating a strong, accurate digital presence around a new website that the school controls.
